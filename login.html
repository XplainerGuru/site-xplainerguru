Authentication Guide

Follow these instructions to set up your account or apply for roles.

FOR STUDENTS:

You can instantly log in using Continue with Google. Alternatively, click "Sign Up Here" to create an email/password account.

FOR TEAM & WRITERS:
  • Click on Want to join our Team? Apply Here at the bottom.
  • Fill out the dedicated careers form. Your account will be pending until Admin approval.
FOR PARENTS / GUARDIANS:
  • Select "Parent" on the Sign Up page.
  • You must provide your Child's System Student ID (e.g., XG-STU-123456).
