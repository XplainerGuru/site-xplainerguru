How to Create an Account

Follow these instructions to set up your account or apply for a special role.

FOR STUDENTS:

Click on "Sign Up Here", fill in your Name, Email, and Password, and click "Create Account". Your email must be verified before you can log in.

FOR TEAM MEMBERS (Mentors/Support/Writers):
  • Click "Apply for Special Role" at the bottom of the Sign Up form.
  • You must be at least 15+ years old.
  • Mandatory 10-digit WhatsApp Number.
  • Strict Rule: Provide a solid reason explaining your background. Admin will review and approve.
  • Team Writers are official members of the platform creating core content.
FOR STUDENT ARTICLE WRITERS:
  • Select "Student Article Writer" under Special Roles.
  • This is a separate role from Team Writers. You contribute articles as a student to the community.
  • Provide a reason for wanting to write.
FOR PARENTS / GUARDIANS:
  • Select "Parent / Guardian" under Special Roles.
  • You must be at least 18+ years old.
  • You must provide your Child's System Student ID (e.g., XG-STU-123456).
